it('should explain who I am')PASS

I test software, then I explain what I found.

I've spent 1.5+ years in QA, mostly on mobile apps, APIs and AI/RAG features. Three habits shape how I work.

01 / tester

I test past the happy path

Most of my week is manual and exploratory testing: writing test cases from PRDs, checking APIs in Postman, and logging what breaks in Jira. Everyone already tried the happy path, so I spend my time on the rest.

02 / ux

I test for the person using it

A screen can match the spec and still confuse people. I report the unclear error message, the dead end, and the flow that takes six taps when two would do.

03 / storyteller

I write bugs as short stories

What I did, what I expected, what happened. If a developer has to message me to understand the report, I wrote it badly. I use the same habit when I post about QA on LinkedIn.
